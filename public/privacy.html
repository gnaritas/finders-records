Privacy

This page is deliberately short, because we don’t collect much.

  • The contact form. Your name, phone, email and message are sent to Finders Records so the shop can reply to you. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • No trackers or cookies. This site sets no advertising cookies and loads no third-party tracking scripts. There is no analytics network between you and the racks.
  • The map. The directions map on the visit page is served by Google, who may set their own cookies when you interact with it.
  • Your say. Ask at the counter or write to findersrecords71@gmail.com to have a message-note deleted.
