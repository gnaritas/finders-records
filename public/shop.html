I

Books are coming to the racks

Used books and select new releases are arriving now — the first books Finders ever carried. Greg kept it music; the new hands are adding a book wall and, down the road, a small stage for acoustic sets and DJs beside the vinyl.

II

The back room

There are still roughly 40,000 CDs in the back room waiting to be catalogued. They hit the racks a few crates at a time — which is exactly the point of coming in and digging.

III

Selling a collection?

Finders now buys used vinyl — for the first time in its fifty-year run. Bring the collection to the counter during open hours, or send a note ahead through the contact page and we’ll tell you when to come.
