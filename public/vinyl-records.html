The biggest room

Vinyl — new, used, out of print

Rows upon rows of it, exactly where they’ve been since 1971. Opening-week customers called it the biggest record selection in Wood and Lucas counties — and they came ready to argue about that.

∞

The genres

Classic rock, metal, pop, soundtracks, hip-hop — the racks cover it all, new pressings and well-loved used copies side by side. “A little bit of everything” is what the staff promises and customers keep confirming.

OUT

Out of print

Hunted an album for years? That’s what a fifty-year-old inventory is for: out-of-print and hard-to-find titles that no streaming queue will ever hand you.

$$

We buy used vinyl

For the first time in Finders’ history, the shop buys records outright. Bring the box from the attic to the counter during open hours — or send a note and we’ll tell you when to come.

Why people flip the racks

“The simplicity of it, the old-school part of it — and the artwork on the front,” one opening-week buyer told the Blade, already holding a Mac Miller and a Kanye pick. A customer of fifty years came back just to see if anything had changed. It hadn’t — and that was the whole point.

The best way to shop Finders is the old way: walk in with no plan, follow the alphabet, and buy whatever grabs you.
