The story so far

Same sign. Same aisles. New hands.

Finders opened on Main Street in 1971. Greg Halamay ran it for more than fifty years, building a stellar reputation for a deep music selection, until he retired in 2023. The store sat quiet for three years — a time capsule with the records still on the shelves.

In 2026 Bowling Green native Broc Curry — who owns Your Media Exchange in Toledo and Ann Arbor and runs the Cla-Zel theatre across Wooster Street — bought the business and brought Finders back. The logo, the vibe and the deep music selection are intact; the new hands add used records bought outright, used books, and plans for a small stage with acoustic sets and DJs beside the vinyl.

“It’s wonderfully overwhelming… it looks a lot like it used to. Very familiar, a very diverse selection.”— a Bowling Green customer, opening week (The Blade)

On reopening morning the line stretched past the corner. It has been coming through the door ever since.

Hours right now

Limited hours while the racks come back online. Extended hours begin in December, and the grand opening is planned for early 2027. Call (419) 352-7677 before you head over — it beats a wasted trip.
