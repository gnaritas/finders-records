Every format, every decade

CDs, cassettes & the retro shelf

Finders spent decades as “Finders Records and Tapes” — the tapes are part of the name’s DNA. Today the whole format wall is back: CDs, cassettes, DVDs, Blu-rays, 4K discs, VHS — and the hardware to play every bit of it.

Forty thousand CDs, back room and all

When the store closed in 2023, the inventory stayed put — rows of records, the CDs alphabetized on the wall, and roughly 40,000 more discs in the back room waiting to be catalogued. Three years later, they’re being worked out onto the floor crate by crate.

The new owners expect CDs to be the top-sellers (vinyl has run steady for twenty years — there’s room for both). And because the whole back room came with the deed, the odds of walking out with something you didn’t come in for are extremely good.
